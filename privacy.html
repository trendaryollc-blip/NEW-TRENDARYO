Section 03 · The red lines

Four things we will never do

These aren't aspirations - they're constraints the business model is built around. If a future feature required breaking one of them, the feature would be dropped, not the promise.

🚫

Sell or rent your data

No data brokerage, no "trusted partners", no exceptions. Products are the business; data is not.

💰

Take money for placement

The shelf has zero paid placements, so there is nothing your data could be sold to influence - the ranking is rating × review volume, and that's all it can ever be.

🕶️

Follow you across the web

No advertising cookies, no social pixels, no cross-site identifiers. What you browse on other sites is your business.

📦

Keep secrets from you

Every storage key is named and explained on the Cookie Policy page - including the ones that only hold demo data.

Section 05 · Nothing lives forever

The retention schedule

Every category from the ledger has an expiry date. The clock starts when the data is created - not when you last logged in.

Deletion requests cut the line: 30 days, maximum
Day 0
An order is placed

The order record is created and payment metadata is stored alongside it. Your receipt email goes out within minutes.

30 days
Abandoned carts age out

Cart and search-history keys are wiped from your browser. Nothing was on our servers to begin with.

12 months
Security logs aggregate

Sign-in telemetry (IP + timestamp) is aggregated beyond recognition. What's left can't point back to you.

24 months
Support conversations purge

Chat and email threads are deleted unless they're tied to an open return or warranty claim.

7 years
Order records close

The tax law that required us to keep orders finally lets go. The record is anonymised, then erased.

Section 06 · You hold the pen

Your rights, and how to use them

Every right below is exercised the same way: one email to privacy@trendaryo.com from your registered address. No forms, no fees, no dark patterns - a human answers within 30 days, usually far sooner.

01

Access

Get a full copy of everything tied to your email address.

How: ask for "a copy" - it arrives as a readable document.
02

Correct

Fix a misspelled name, an old address, a wrong phone number.

How: reply to any order email with the correction.
03

Delete

Erase your account, saved addresses and order history.

How: email from your registered address; done within 30 days.
04

Port

Take your order history with you when you go.

How: say "export" - it arrives as a CSV file.
05

Object

Switch off every marketing email in one click, keep your account.

How: Email Preferences or the footer link.
06

Complain

Escalate anything above to a person, not a queue.

How: mark your email "escalation" - it jumps the line.
