Section 01 · The two mechanisms

Cookies vs localStorage

Two different storage mechanisms, two very different levels of invasiveness. This site leans almost entirely on the gentle one.

🍪

Cookies

Small text files that your browser sends back to a server on every request - the classic mechanics behind sessions and, infamously, cross-site advertising. Trendaryo sets no advertising or tracking cookies. When you sign in, your session is handled by Firebase Auth tokens, which exist to keep you signed in and nothing else.

🗄️

localStorage

A larger store that stays on your device and is only read by the pages that wrote it. This is where Trendaryo keeps almost everything - and it's why there is no consent banner on this site: nothing here needs one, because nothing leaves your device until you press Pay.

Section 03 · Requests that leave this site

Third-party services

Four services receive a request from your browser when you use Trendaryo. None of them is an ad network.

🔤

Google Fonts

Your browser fetches the Orbitron and Rajdhani font files from Google's CDN. Google sees the request - your IP and user agent, as with any web asset - and this site sets no cookies for it.

🖼️

Unsplash

Product and hero photography loads from Unsplash's CDN under the same rules: a plain image request, no identifiers attached by us.

🔑

Firebase Auth

Handles sign-in sessions when you choose to sign in. It sees your email and a hashed credential - and nothing about your browsing. Guest shoppers never touch it.

💳

Payment processor

Only at checkout. Card details travel directly from your browser to the processor over TLS; our servers receive a result code, never a card number.

Section 04 · The manual route

Browser controls

If you'd rather use your browser's own tools, every mainstream one can clear site data - here's where the switch lives.

Chrome
Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data

Or Settings → Privacy → Third-party cookies / Site settings → View permissions and data stored across sites → pick trendaryo.com.

Firefox
Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

"Manage Data" lets you remove storage per site - search for trendaryo and remove it.

Safari
Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Per-site removal, including local storage, in one panel.

DevTools
Application → Storage → Local Storage

The power route: see exactly which keys from Section 02 exist on your device before you clear anything. The ledger makes no claims you can't check.

Section 05 · Erase in one click

The wipe button

The honest way to prove Section 02 was complete: press it, refresh, and check DevTools - the keys are gone.

Clear this site's local data

Removes every key from the ledger above: your cart, wishlist, recently viewed, order cache, search history, reviews, theme and newsletter flag. You will be signed out if you were signed in - sign back in from the account menu afterwards. Your account itself is untouched; this only clears what lives in this browser.

Section 06 · A signal with nothing to do

Do Not Track

Your browser's Do Not Track signal asks every site to stop tracking you. On Trendaryo it changes nothing - there are no trackers to switch off. That's not compliance theatre; it's the design of the store. The same promise the privacy policy makes in Section 03.
