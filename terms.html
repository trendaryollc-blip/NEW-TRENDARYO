Section 04 · From cart to contract

How an order forms - and when it binds

Four moments, one contract. The moment that counts is marked.

Step 1
The cart

Browsing, shortlisting and filling the cart owe nobody anything. Your cart lives in your browser until you check out.

Step 2
The request

At checkout you submit an address and a payment method. That's an offer to buy - we can still decline it (see the price-error rule below).

Step 3
The confirmation email - this is the contract

The moment it lands, we're bound: it lists your order number, items, prices and delivery window. The 30-day return clock starts at delivery, not at this email.

Step 4
Dispatch

Orders are packed within 24 hours on business days and tracking activates as soon as the carrier scans the parcel.

Pricing, in four lines: prices are in USD · promotions apply the way the promotion says (Section 08) · stock and prices can change between visits · and if a listing is mispriced by an obvious margin - a $299 item showing $29 - we flag it before charging. You can cancel free or re-order at the correct price; we never quietly charge a wrong number.

Section 07 · The 30-day promise

Returns & refunds

The short rules behind the numbers the trust strip shows on every page. Full detail lives on the returns page and the refund policy.

Start a return: return-request.html
30 days
The window

From delivery, on any unused item in its original packaging with all accessories. Change of mind counts - no interrogation required.

Label
Who pays the postage

Faulty, damaged or wrong item: we pay, and a prepaid label lands in your email within 24 hours. Change of mind: you pay, and a tracked service is recommended.

2 days
Inspection

Your return is inspected within 2 business days of arriving at the warehouse. Approval is the norm; if there's an issue, we contact you with photos and reasoning.

5-7 days
The refund

Issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days of a passed inspection, with an email the moment it's processed.

The short exceptions list, in full: personalised or custom-made items, opened beauty and personal care, intimate apparel, items marked Final Sale, and gift cards. Everything else on the shelf follows the 30-day rule.

Section 09 · Keep the shelf honest

Acceptable use

A shortlist store only works if its signals are real. These rules protect the ranking you rely on.

??

No bulk scraping

Browsing freely: yes. Harvesting the catalogue with automated tools, or reselling access to it: no. Ask first - some uses are fine with permission.

??

No fake orders or reviews

Reviews feed the ranking directly - faking them poisons the shelf for everyone. We remove fake reviews and suspend the accounts behind them.

???

No interference

No attacking the site, probing for vulnerabilities without disclosure, or disrupting other customers' checkout. Responsible disclosure of a security issue is always welcome and never punished.

??

No unauthorised reselling

Buying the shelf to flip it elsewhere undermines the curation. Reseller partnerships exist - the affiliate program is the honest way to earn from Trendaryo.
