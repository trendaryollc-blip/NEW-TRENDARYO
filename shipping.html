Know before you order

Shipping Policies

📦 Free Shipping, Always

Shipping cost is included in every product price. What you see on the product page is what you pay (plus tax where applicable). No minimum spend, no promo code needed.

⏱️ Processing Times

  • Orders are placed with our fulfillment partner within 24–48 business hours
  • Weekend orders start processing Monday
  • Tracking activates when the carrier first scans the parcel (can lag handover by 1–3 days)
  • Items in one order may ship separately from different facilities

🛡️ Insurance & Signature

  • Every shipment is insured at no extra cost
  • Orders over $200 require a signature on delivery
  • Lost or damaged? We reship or refund within 48 hours of confirmation

🗺️ International Shipping

We ship to over 50 countries. International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes set by your country — these are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in our prices. Tracking is available for every international shipment.

📍 Real-Time Tracking

You get a tracking number by email the moment your order ships. Use it on our Track Order page or directly on the carrier's site for live updates.

📬 Delivery Addresses

  • We ship to the address you enter at checkout — double-check it before paying
  • P.O. Box deliveries depend on the carrier; some lines require a street address
  • If you are not home, the carrier leaves a retry notice or delivers to a local pickup point

Customs & duties

International orders may incur local customs charges. These are set by your country and are not included in our prices.

All delivery times are estimates, never guaranteed dates. Allow 24–48 business hours of processing before the window starts — the window begins from dispatch, not from the date of order. Items in one order may arrive in separate parcels from different facilities. Carrier delays, customs clearance and remote postcodes can extend delivery windows. Contact support if your order is significantly late.
