Know before you order

Shipping Policies

?? International Shipping

We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the buyer's responsibility. Tracking is available for every international shipment.

?? Dispatch Times

  • Orders placed before 3 PM ship same day
  • Orders after 3 PM ship next business day
  • Weekend orders ship Monday
  • Express and overnight get priority packing

?? Insurance & Signature

  • Every shipment is insured at no extra cost
  • Orders over $200 require signature on delivery
  • Lost or damaged? We reship or refund within 48 hours

?? Real-Time Tracking

You get a tracking number the moment your order ships. Use it on our Track Order page or directly on the carrier's site for live updates.

Customs & duties

International orders may incur local customs charges. These are set by your country and are not included in our prices.

All delivery times are estimates and begin from the date of dispatch, not the date of order. Carrier delays outside our control may extend delivery windows. Contact support if your order is significantly late.
